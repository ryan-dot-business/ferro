Service buildings, transmission support, substations, and other infrastructure that support critical energy systems.

The challenge

Complex Challenges

Energy and utility projects carry a different level of responsibility and impact. Whether it’s a dam, substation, control building, transmission segment, generation facility, or remote service site, the work is often shaped by layered compliance requirements, extended asset life expectations, challenging logistics, and multiple project stakeholders with their own concerns and priorities. In a sector that leaves no room for interpretation errors or documentation gaps, every structure needs to satisfy the authorities reviewing it and perform reliably to support the systems inside it.

Our approach

Strong Solutions

Ferro Building Systems supports energy and utility projects with engineering, consulting, manufacturing, and coordination expertise to deliver structures that excel in demanding environments. We help project teams define what the structure needs to do, and then we align everything else: the building systems, the documentation, QA/QC processes, and the installation support. By getting involved at the ground level, we collaborate early with utility providers, EPCs, contractors, engineers, and consultants to reduce uncertainty before it leads to rework, delays, and additional costs.

How we help

  • 02

    Remote Site Support

    Energy and utility facilities and infrastructure are often located where access, logistics, and labour availability create additional pressure. We account for those realities from the start, coordinating building systems and delivery solutions tailored to the site's conditions and the path to it.

  • 03

    Compliant Coordination

    Projects across this sector need to undergo extensive, detailed review processes, often with strict documentation requirements. It’s a complex process, but we help teams work within those requirements by maintaining clear scope alignment and ironclad documentation processes to keep approvals and execution on track.

  • 04

    Specialized Systems

    Structures within this sector require more than a standard frame, roof, and wall package. We account for specific project needs, such as openings, crane loads, equipment access, envelope performance, coatings, insulation, fall protection considerations, and other specialized system requirements tied to the structure’s use.

The challenge

Complex Challenges

Waste and water facilities are essential. Communities, municipalities, and industrial operations rely on them every day, and if they’re not functioning properly, everyone feels the impact. Whether our structure is part of a water treatment plant, wastewater facility, waste management operation, or a maintenance building, the work comes with tight site constraints, demanding work environments, public accountability, and long-term performance expectations. From access and ventilation considerations, every small detail can have a massive impact on performance in the field.

Our approach

Strong Solutions

We support waste and water projects with metal building consulting, engineering, manufacturing, and coordination expertise tailored to complex operating demands. Working alongside project teams, we define what the structure needs to house, protect, and support, and then align the building system around those requirements. By doing our part early, we help owners, contractors, engineers, and consultants account for site conditions, envelope performance, equipment access, approvals, and long-term maintenance needs before fabrication begins. The result is a structure built around site realities, not just drawings.

How we help

How We Support
Waste & Water

    Tailored Systems

    Constructability Review

Through design-assist input, constructability reviews, budget analysis, and system coordination, we address major and minor details to ensure there are no conflicts. The challenge

Complex Challenges

Aviation and transport facilities often call for steel buildings that do more than provide covered space. Hangars, support buildings, vehicle maintenance areas, and other structures need to account for large clear spans, custom openings, vehicle and equipment movement, site access, and systems that keep daily work moving. These projects can also involve tight schedules, active operating environments, and coordination with existing infrastructure. That means every structure needs to be planned around practical use and the site constraints that can affect performance once the building is up and running.

Our approach

Strong Solutions

Our team supports aviation and transport projects as consultants who understand the engineering, manufacturing, and installation requirements and how they need to be adjusted to suit the facility's operational needs. From large openings and envelope systems to structural loads, access requirements, and installation considerations, we help project teams work through details before fabrication starts. This foundational planning helps owners, contractors, engineers, and other project teams reduce uncertainty and keep the structure aligned with the site, schedule, and other operational needs.

How we help

How We Keep
Projects Moving

Aviation and transport buildings need to support movement, clearance, and unique access requirements. We bring practical design input, deep system knowledge, and coordinated project support to deliver metal buildings that provide steady performance in dynamic, demanding environments.

  • 01

    Large Spans & Clearances

    Hangars and other transport facilities need a large amount of open interior space to support clear movement paths and coordinated workflows. Through early planning, we account for span requirements, overhead clearance needs, equipment access considerations, and other crucial details that shape how the building functions.

  • 02

    Specialized Openings

    Large doors, access points, framed openings, and envelope details directly impact performance and usability. We help coordinate these elements from the start so the building supports the movement of everything inside it, from aircraft and vehicles to equipment, people, and materials.

    Infrastructure Coordination

    These facilities are often built or expanded around existing infrastructure, active operations, access routes, and other complex site constraints. We ensure teams are informed of those realities before they create conflicts in planning, fabrication, delivery, or installation.

  • 04

    Preconstruction Support

    The most important work happens before physical construction starts. Through design-assist input, full constructability reviews, budget considerations, and system coordination, we reduce risk before anything reaches the site. It’s a method that adds value at every stage, not just up front.

Commercial

Functional space, adaptable designs, efficient delivery, and cost-effective quality.

The challenge

Complex Challenges

Commercial projects need to balance function, budget, appearance, schedule, and long-term usability. Some buildings just need a large open space for operations. But others? They need offices, storefront glazing, mezzanines, overhead doors, customer-facing finishes, or room to expand as the business grows. That mix makes early planning paramount. A commercial steel building should not only fit the site and budget, but support how the business operates, how employees and customers move through the space, and how the structure may need to change over time.

Our approach

Strong Solutions

To build for business, we provide pre-engineered building systems that are practical, flexible, and scalable as needs and situations change. We work closely with project teams to align the structure, envelope, openings, finishes, and accessories with the intended use of the space. Whether the project is a new facility, an expansion, a shop, a warehouse, a mixed-use business space, or a customer-facing building, our role is to turn commercial requirements into consistently reliable structures that support the operations within them.

How we help

Comprehensive Support
for Commercial Projects

Effective commercial buildings need to support workflows, access requirements, visual expectations, future plans, and the realities of day-to-day use. We bring the technical insight and system knowledge to help project teams make informed decisions at every stage of the build.

  • 01

    Adaptable Layouts

    Far from rigid, pre-engineered steel buildings are flexible enough to support open floor areas, second-storey mezzanines, offices, shops, warehouse space, and other configurations as needed. Once we’ve determined how the space will be used every day, we coordinate systems that support efficient, functional performance.

  • 02

    Openings & Access

    Overhead doors, standard doors, bay doors, windows, storefront glazing, large framed openings, and service access points all shape how a commercial building works. We coordinate these components to ensure movement, visibility, natural light, and operational flow are built in from the ground up.

  • 03

    Appearance & Exteriors

    Unlike many other sectors, commercial buildings need to balance practical use with a more stylized or brand-aligned exterior. We consider wall panels, roof systems, parapets, canopies, and other exterior details to ensure the final building meets functional needs without missing visual expectations or brand image.

  • 04

    Expansion-Ready

    A business’s needs can change quickly, so it needs a space that can adapt to them. Metal buildings can include additions, expandable end walls, future access points, and other structural planning that can make future expansions or other adjustments more practical and efficient.

Complex Challenges

Our approach

Strong Solutions

We consider every variable that can influence agricultural buildings, selecting pre-engineered systems that support efficient function and lasting durability. From more straightforward storage buildings to specialized production and conservation structures, we align the frame, envelope, openings, insulation, and everything else with the operation's requirements. We’re here to bring practical guidance to the details that matter, adding clarity without complicating the project. The result is a structure that supports productivity, protects people and assets, and performs reliably day after day.

How We Support
Agricultural Builds

The challenge

Complex Challenges

True to their name, industrial buildings need industrial-grade strength to support the work happening inside. That work could be production, loading, maintenance, equipment repair, manufacturing, fabrication, or other operations that place heavy demands on the structure and systems. These projects often involve far more than a standard solution. Crane loads, specialized openings, equipment clearances, existing building connections, corrosion considerations, and tight timelines are just some of the challenges that can influence how the building is designed, supplied, and supported.

Our approach

Strong Solutions

Ferro Building Systems supports industrial projects with pre-engineered metal buildings shaped around function, performance, compliance, and practical use. We collaborate with project teams to define what the structure needs to carry, protect, house, and accommodate, then we align the frame, envelope, openings, and other elements around those requirements. Since industrial scopes can change quickly, early clarity is crucial. We help owners, engineers, contractors, and consultants identify potential pain points, coordinate key systems, and reduce the risk of costly changes once construction starts.

How we help

Support and Insight
for Industrial Builds

Industrial projects need more than the strength of steel. They need precise planning and careful coordination to keep operations moving and ensure standards are met. With our combination of technical insight, practical solutions, and industry expertise, we help teams plan smarter and build stronger.

  • 01

    Load Requirements

    Industrial buildings often need to accommodate overhead cranes, heavy equipment, suspended platforms, mezzanines, and other load-specific requirements, so we coordinate all structural systems around those exact demands. The result is a space that supports strong performance and consistent safety.

  • 02

    Process-Driven Layouts

    A well-planned industrial building gives teams enough room to move, maintain, load, store, and adapt. Ferro helps align the structure with the realities of day-to-day operations, from equipment placement and circulation to clearances, usable floor area, and future plans.

  • 03

    Constraint Considerations

    Many industrial projects involve expanding or connecting to existing facilities, where height, slope, foundation, wall system, and general site limitations come into play. We find the best path forward through careful planning and precise system coordination, ensuring cleaner, more efficient integration with existing systems.

  • 04

    Durable by Design

    Sites and surrounding environments can place additional demands on the building envelope, materials, finishes, and coatings. Since 2005

Building a
Better Way

Ferro Building Systems was founded in 2005 by Jack & Molly Hebert. After spending two decades in the industry, Jack noticed several practices and tactics that didn’t sit right with him. He knew there was a better way. And if there wasn’t, he’d build one.

So, he and his small team set out to bring a higher level of planning, technical skill, collaboration, transparency, and accountability to pre-engineered metal buildings. It was a foundation built on not just understanding what was being built, but also why.

Today, Maxime, Joel, and Patrick, having grown alongside the business for over 15 years, are carrying that same vision into the future and leading Ferro as a trusted partner across highly regulated, performance-driven industries.

Services

Integrated Systems

Pre-engineered metal buildings often incorporate integrated systems to meet specific functional requirements. Ferro’s here to help teams select, coordinate, supply, and install the systems that support each structure’s performance and long-term reliability.

Our approach

Systems Built Around
the Work They Support

Every building system has a part to play. Frame systems carry the loads. Wall and roof panels protect everything inside. Openings, accessories, and additions support long-term safety, efficiency, and durability.

We bring a consultative, collaborative approach to the early stages of your project, helping your team build a unified solution that addresses the full scope of requirements rather than a fragmented one that treats systems as separate pieces.

01 · Energy performance

Insulated Metal Panels (IMPs)

IMPs combine exterior cladding, insulation, and an interior liner in a single panel, giving the envelope the thermal performance it needs to meet energy requirements. We help select profiles, thicknesses, and details to suit the building’s use and energy targets.

Request the IMP handout

02 · Energy performance

Composite Wall & Roof Systems

An alternative route to an energy rating. Composite systems pair metal panels with insulation and liner systems, so the envelope reaches its target while giving teams flexibility on cost, finishes, and installation.

Request the composite handout

03 · Structure

Hybrid Steel Systems

Hybrid systems blend conventional structural steel with the pre-engineered approach during design, so multi-level office or commercial spaces can be attached to, or built into, a pre-engineered building.

Request the hybrid steel handout

04 · Space

Mezzanines

Mezzanines expand the usable square footage inside the building’s footprint for offices, storage, or equipment platforms, coordinated with the frame from the start so loads, stairs, and clearances work together.

Request the mezzanine handout

05 · Material handling

Cranes

Overhead and jib cranes keep material moving through the building. We design the frame around crane loads and clearances, and can supply and install the crane systems too. For Axton’s fabrication expansion, that meant two 20-ton cranes and six 1-ton jib cranes.

Request the crane handout

06 · Safety

Fall Arrest Systems

Roof safety lifelines and anchors give crews safe access for maintenance for the life of the building, planned into the roof system rather than added after the fact.

Request the fall arrest handout

Why metal

The Many Benefits
of Metal Buildings

Pre-engineered steel and aluminum buildings are efficient, adaptable, and built for consistent long-term performance. Each structure is shaped around the specific application, loading conditions, site requirements, and applicable codes, giving project teams a practical way to balance speed, cost control, quality, and compliance expectations.

  • 01

    Efficient by Design

    Pre-engineered buildings are optimized from the start, from material selection and fabrication processes to the final delivery. This helps teams minimize material waste, improve coordination, and maintain project schedules without impacting quality.

  • 02

    Built for Every Demand

    Steel and aluminum buildings can be designed to meet the most demanding structural requirements, including large openings, overhead crane systems, mezzanines, heavy point loads, specialized equipment, and future expansion needs.

  • 03

    Long-Term Performance

    Steel and aluminum offer lasting durability, recyclability, and low maintenance requirements, making metal buildings a strong fit for facilities that need to maintain reliable and steady performance for years, even in harsh environments.

  • 04

    Versatile and Flexible

    Far from rigid, metal buildings can be flexibly shaped into utilitarian infrastructure buildings or more architecturally inclined commercial facilities. This is achieved through in-depth layout mapping, a range of finish options, and planning for future additions.

Services

Preconstruction Services

Through our preconstruction services, we help your team clarify requirements, assess feasibility, improve overall constructability, and make informed decisions before metal moves into fabrication and construction begins. It’s how we set the foundation and framework to support better work every step of the way.

Why it matters

Better Buildings Start
Before Construction

Before anything is built, it needs to be fully understood. That’s especially true for pre-engineered metal buildings, as they leave little to no room for unresolved details once fabrication and installation begin.

We help owners, contractors, engineers, and consultants work through the important details early, from scope and budget to code requirements, building systems, site constraints, and long-term performance.

Design-build

Design-Build Support
for the Entire Structure

Design-build works best when the right technical partners are involved and aligned from the start. By combining both design and construction under one roof, we provide metal building expertise that clarifies the building’s intended use, performance requirements, budget considerations, schedule concerns, and complex site conditions well before anything is built. Through this approach, we:

  • 01

    Close Gaps Early

    We help connect design intent with applicable building requirements and actual site realities, reducing the gaps between what’s planned and what’s practical.

  • 02

    Keep Costs Controlled

    By reviewing systems, materials, loads, openings, and site requirements early, we help identify opportunities to control costs without compromising performance.

  • 03

    Reduce Project Risk

    Once a pre-engineered metal building enters fabrication, changes are difficult and costly to implement. Our process ensures every detail is correct, so no changes are needed.

How we add value

Value Engineering

Adding value to a project means finding smarter, more efficient ways to meet the project’s technical, operational, and budget requirements without compromising quality. It’s never easy, but it’s always well worth the effort.

Constructability Reviews

We assess and inform how the building will come together in the field, helping teams reduce installation challenges long before materials arrive on site. That means smoother, more efficient installs and less rework.

System Selection

We help select frame, wall, roof, insulation, and other systems that align with the structure’s intended use, operating environment, and long-term performance needs, resulting in better performance and reduced maintenance costs.

Cost Control

Costs can get out of control quickly. To keep them consistent and transparent, we look for practical efficiencies in design, materials, sequencing, and the overall scope to keep budgets aligned and potentially reduce expenses.

Schedule Awareness

We work alongside project teams early to clarify fabrication timelines, procurement realities, approvals, and delivery requirements before they impact delivery or cause delays that increase costs and labour requirements.

Risk Reduction

By clarifying compliance requirements and outlining key site considerations from the start, we help prevent minor assumptions or overlooked details from becoming major field issues or safety risks for project teams.
